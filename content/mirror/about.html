Transforming Medical Documentation Worldwide

All about how we build the next chapter in healthcare technology

Who we are

Chartnote is the productivity tool created to bring back the joy of practicing medicine.

Stay in control

Medical documentation is one of the main contributors to burnout. As a clinician, completing chart notes while treating patients can be time-consuming and an epic challenge.

Make technology work for you

Transition from paper to EHR documentation placed a significant burden on clinicians. Chartnote enables clinicians to focus on their patient and prevent them from becoming another professional data entry clerk.

Meaningful interaction

Chartnote was developed to address a growing problem that EHRs helped create: clinicians spending more time in front of the computer instead of interacting with their patients.

"Less than half a century ago doctors used index cards as a medical record tool. They used to have longer conversations and more meaningful interactions with their patients...

Our goal is to bring back the joy of practicing medicine by creating a solution that enables clinicians to effortlessly capture the essentials of the patient visit in a simple and efficient way."

Gerardo Guerra Bonilla, MD
Chartnote Founder and CEO
