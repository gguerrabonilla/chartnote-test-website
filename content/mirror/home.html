Patient Encounter
Recording
Dr
Hello. What brings you in today?
Pt
I have been having chest pain for two days.
Dr
Any shortness of breath?
Pt
Yes, specially when the pain happens.
Dr
Can you describe the pain?
Pt
It feels tight, in the center of my chest. Worse when I walk upstairs.
AI-Generated SOAP Note
S

Subjective

55 y/o female, chest pain x 2 days, "heavy pressure", worse with exertion, + dyspnea.

O

Objective

BP 155/90, HR 98, SpO2 96%. CV: RRR, no murmurs. Lungs: CTA bilaterally.

A

Assessment

Acute chest pain - r/o ACS, PE. Risk factors: HTN, HLD.

P

Plan

STAT EKG, troponin, CBC. ASA 324mg. Telemetry. Reassess.
