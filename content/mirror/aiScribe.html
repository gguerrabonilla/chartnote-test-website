AI Scribe

Turn patient conversations into structured clinical notes in seconds.

Chartnote seamlessly captures and converts your patient interactions into precise, ready-to-use documentation.

Revolutionize your medical documentation.

Chartnote AI Scribe is an advanced artificial intelligence system designed to streamline the process of creating medical notes based on clinician-patient encounters.

How it works?

1

Speak naturally

Dictate naturally using your computer or mobile device.

2

AI transcribes

AI creates a polished structured note.

3

Review & sign

Edit, refine, and transfer into your EHR in seconds.

What makes Voice Chart different

Voice Chart goes beyond traditional dictation. Instead of just converting speech into text, it understands clinical context and transforms your voice into structured, ready-to-use notes—so you spend less time editing and more time with your patients.

Save 1+ hour per day.

Focus on your patient, not your screen.

Reduce documentation fatigue.

Maintain consistent, high-quality notes.

What makes AI Scribe different

Natural dictation

AI Scribe records, transcribes, and converts your patient visits so you can focus on what matters.

HIPAA compliant. SOC 2 Type II.

Secure storage and transmission for seamless medical documentation, powered by state-of-the-art encryption.

Instant output

Transforming a doctor's appointment into comprehensive clinical notes is now incredibly simple. You only need your mobile device.

Works in your EHR

Chartnote's Chrome extension lets you dictate directly into any web-based EHR—turning thoughts into notes without disruption.
