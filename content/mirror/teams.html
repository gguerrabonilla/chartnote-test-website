Chartnote for Teams

AI-driven healthcare documentation that delivers.

Streamline documentation, elevate care.

Chartnote's AI-driven platform transforms the Clinical Documentation Improvement (CDI) process, empowering clinicians to efficiently produce comprehensive and accurate documentation. This transformation not only enhances patient care but also optimizes healthcare delivery and timeliness of required documentation.

Empower your clinical team with Chartnote

Maximize reimbursement, minimize denials.

With superior documentation quality, Chartnote ensures clinical notes accurately reflect the level of care provided, supporting appropriate reimbursement and reducing denials. This clarity and completeness directly impact your organization's revenue stream and compliance posture.

Empower clinicians, reduce burnout.

Chartnote significantly reduces the time clinicians spend on documentation, allowing them to dedicate more time to patient care. This efficiency not only enhances the quality and timeliness of completed notes but also plays a crucial role in increasing revenue for healthcare organizations.

Safeguarding every piece of data

Chartnote is a bastion of data security, ensuring the utmost safety for your information. Our dedication is proven through rigorous third-party compliance certifications and a strict policy against data selling, advertising use, or training third-party models.

Why Chartnote?

Management tools

Simplify your team's administration with our suite of management tools. Enjoy unified billing and an intuitive dashboard to effortlessly manage users. Overseeing your team's Chartnote usage is straightforward.

HIPAA compliant

Chartnote ensures your patient's privacy with top-tier data protection and a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement, maintaining strict standards for secure, collaborative patient documentation.

Premium support

Ease into Chartnote with our guided implementation and onboarding process, designed to integrate seamlessly with your team's workflow.

Start using AI Scribe today.

Download our mobile app to capture your patient encounter, dictate and craft notes on the go. Sync your mobile device to any computer to utilize it as a microphone.
