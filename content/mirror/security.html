Privacy. That's Chartnote

Chartnote prioritizes privacy in healthcare, rigorously adhering to HIPAA standards. Its platform embodies a privacy-forward approach, designed to meet the stringent demands of modern healthcare enterprises.

Our Stance on Data Privacy and Security

Chartnote prioritizes data privacy and security with a robust framework, ensuring brand safety and trust through comprehensive protection, making it a trusted partner in clinical documentation.

HIPAA Compliance

Ensuring patient data is handled with utmost confidentiality. Sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) and confidently use Chartnote, knowing your data handling meets strict compliance standards.

Independent data security audits

Ongoing security monitoring by third-party firm to guarantee our security measures are always up-to date and effective.

Encrypted data handling

Your data is secure with Chartnote's end-to-end encryption on AWS, adhering to the highest privacy and security standards, ensuring complete confidentiality and compliance.
